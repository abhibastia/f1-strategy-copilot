01Was it a wet race, or just a wet day?

The obvious question for this dataset is whether rain makes a race chaotic. Measured rainfall alone says it barely does — and the reason turns out to be the most interesting thing in the project.

Across 59 races, going from any race to 15 mm or more of rain moves the retirement rate only from {{ thresholds[0].dnf_pct }}% to {{ thresholds[-1].dnf_pct }}%. Daily rainfall is a weak predictor of a chaotic race.

Why the split. The weather archive reports a daily total. It cannot distinguish rain that fell overnight from rain that fell during the race. The narrative can — Monza 2024 saw the most rain of any race here and stayed calm because the report notes only “a greater chance of rain than initially forecast”, while São Paulo ran “in rainy conditions on a wet track”. Neither source is trustworthy alone. That is why this project embeds race reports instead of only querying results.
